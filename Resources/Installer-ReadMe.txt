Luma 0.2.0 — Development Build

macOS 26 or later · Apple Silicon

Drag Luma to Applications, or open the app from its existing Downloads folder.

Luma follows your System appearance by default. Light and Dark overrides are
available in the toolbar and Settings. Live Mac information appears in the
menu bar; hide it in Settings if you prefer. Closing the main window keeps
the menu monitor running. Choose Quit Luma to stop the app completely.

Scans run locally and cleanup always requires a review. Generated caches,
application data, and personal files remain separate. Cleanup moves selected
items to the macOS Trash and records their paths in Activity.

This development build includes expanded browser and package-cache coverage,
scan progress and location coverage, and recovery for interrupted cleanup and
restore operations. The source, tests, product specification, and current
limits are available in the Luma GitHub repository.
